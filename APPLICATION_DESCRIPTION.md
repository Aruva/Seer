## **SouthSeer — League Tracker for Magic & Tabletop Wargames**

SouthSeer is your all-in-one league management bot for Discord. Track MTG Commander (EDH) leagues, 60-card 1v1 matches, wargame tournaments, and maintain rosters—all in one place with our integrated Shop system.

**Features:**
- EDH league ranking and match tracking
- 60-card 1v1 format support
- Wargame league management
- Integrated shop system for managed gameplay
- Automatic leaderboards and season tracking
- Deck and army roster management

Originally built by Amy Troschinetz ([@lexicalunit](https://github.com/lexicalunit)), now enhanced and self-hosted by Southside Studio and Hobbies.
